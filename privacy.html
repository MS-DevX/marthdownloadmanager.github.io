Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 22, 2026

At MDM (Marth Download Manager) and the MDM Browser Extension, we believe privacy is a fundamental right. MDM is built local-first. We do not track you, we do not collect personal information, and we do not sell or monetize your data.

1. No Personal Data Collection

MDM and its browser extension do not collect, transmit, or store personal information such as your name, email, IP address, browsing history, keystrokes, or account credentials.

2. Browser Extension Permissions

The MDM Chrome extension requests permissions solely to capture file downloads and pass them to your local MDM application:

  • downloads: Detects and intercepts file downloads you initiate to accelerate them through the desktop engine.
  • storage: Saves your local extension preferences (such as auto-capture on/off and excluded file types).
  • contextMenus: Provides right-click "Download with MDM" options for links and media.
  • nativeMessaging: Communicates exclusively with the MDM desktop application running on your local machine.
  • webRequest: Inspects media stream requests on web pages you visit to locate downloadable video/audio files.
  • <all_urls>: Allows download interception to function across any website where you choose to download files.

3. Remote Code Policy

The MDM Browser Extension does not use remote code. All code and assets are packaged and executed locally within the extension bundle in strict compliance with Google Chrome Web Store policies.

4. Local Data Storage

All download tasks, file paths, and queue history are saved strictly on your local disk in a local database. No download history or file details are ever uploaded to any remote server or cloud service.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties, advertisers, or tracking networks.

6. Contact
